## About / Introduction

**What it is**
Peer Coaching is a student-run programme at UWCSEA. Trained peer coaches run
regular sessions in the subjects they know well. It's free, it's on campus, and
it's built around learning *how* to study — not just getting an answer.

**Why it exists**
Private tuition in Singapore is expensive and not everyone can access it. Peer
Coaching exists so that a student who is struggling can get consistent,
knowledgeable help from someone who sat the same courses recently — without a
price tag.

**How it works (3 steps)**
1. **Sign up** — tell us the subject you want help with and your free periods.
2. **Get matched** — we pair you with a coach who knows that course and shares a
   free slot.
3. **Meet regularly** — most pairs meet once or twice a week during lunch or
   study periods, through the term.

## FAQ  `[TBC — confirm answers with coordinators]`

**How do I sign up for coaching?**
Fill in the student sign-up form (linked on this site). You'll be asked for your
subject, your current level and your free periods. A coordinator matches you with
a coach within about a week.

**How do I become a peer coach?**
Use the "Become a coach" form. You'll usually need a 6/7 in the subject at your
current level and a recommendation from a teacher. Coaches get a short training
session on how to run a coaching meeting.

**Does it cost anything?**
No. Peer Coaching is completely free. That's the point of the programme.

**How much time does it take?**
Most coaching pairs meet once or twice a week for 30–40 minutes, usually at lunch
or in a shared study period. You agree the schedule with your coach.

**What happens in a session?**
It's not a lecture. Coaches work through problems with you, look at your past
papers, help you plan essays or revision, and share the study habits that worked
for them. You bring what you're stuck on.

**What if it's not working out?**
Tell a coordinator. Re-matching is normal and no one takes it personally —
finding the right fit matters more than sticking with the first pairing.
