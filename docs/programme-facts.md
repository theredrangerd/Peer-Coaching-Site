# Programme facts (sanitised)

Distilled from the coordinators' handout "Peer Coaching Info 2026-27" (kept
locally in `peer-coaching-resources/`, which is **gitignored** because it names
students and lists student email addresses — never commit it, never copy names,
class codes or emails into the public site). This file is the safe, public-OK
summary. Source of truth for what the site may claim.

## Programme
- Run by students, for students; principle of "inclusive knowledge": every
  student deserves to learn somewhere approachable, accessible and tailored.
  Students personalise their learning and take ownership of it.
- Sessions are small: the coachee brings a specific thing they are stuck on.
- Coaches "sat the same papers and studied the same course **a year or two
  ago**" and remember what was hard ("took the same course **last year**").
- 12 coaches, among the best in their subject (owner, 2026-10-04).
- Supported by the Head of Learning (more named staff to come).

## Who and what
- Open to **Grades 9, 10 and 11** as coachees; any number of subjects.
- Subjects (9): Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Economics, English,
  History, Geography, Art.

## Sessions
- Five weekly slots: 7:30–8:00 am (before school); 12:45–1:15 pm and
  1:15–1:45 pm (lunch); 3:00–4:00 pm and 4:00–5:00 pm (after school).
- Coachees sign up in advance for a subject and slot; matched by subject, not
  at random. Session size: coachee plus "zero, one, two or three others".
- Session flow: sign up, come with something specific, work through it
  together, finish with a short exit ticket.

## Expectations and safeguarding (as stated in the handout)
- Turn up for a slot you signed up to, or cancel in advance.
- Coaches support learning; they never complete assessed work. Coaching stops
  where academic honesty begins.
- Confidential: nothing is reported to teachers as a judgement on ability.
  **Exception:** a concern about a student's wellbeing or safety is passed to a
  member of staff — non-negotiable, and every coach is briefed.
- Feedback runs both ways: students give feedback on sessions, coaches record
  how sessions went, coordinators use both to resolve friction.

## Sign-up
- Registration for 2026–27 is open via a Google Form (URL is in the site's
  sign-up buttons).
- Questions go to the two student coordinators. Their names, class codes and
  emails are deliberately **not** published; use role titles and a shared
  programme address (still to be created — see open-questions.md #17).
