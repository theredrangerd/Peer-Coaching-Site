Why Peer Coaching works

Run by students, for students. Your coach isn’t an outside expert. They were recently exactly where you are.

They know your course

Grade 9 and 10 students get a coach who took the course earlier. Grade 11 students get a coach in the same IB course now. Either way, they know what comes up and which topics are harder or easier than they look.

Confident in their subject

Coaches are students who are strong and confident in their subject. They explain clearly and show you how they approach it.

You set the pace

You pick the subject. Bring a question, a topic that didn’t land or a past paper. Or let your coach build you a plan. Nobody moves on before you do, and you don’t need to know what you’re doing before you arrive.

Easy to ask questions

Sessions are small. You may be the only student, or one of up to four. That’s easier than asking in a class of twenty-five.

Small commitment, no cost

A slot is half an hour. It costs a lunch break, not an evening. It’s free, and it works alongside any other support you have.

How a session works

There’s nothing to prepare. Choose how you want to work.

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Pick a subject and slot in advance. You’re matched by subject, not at random.

  2. 2

    Choose your path

    Bring something to work on, or let your coach plan it.

  3. 3

    Work through it

    Your coach works through it with you.

  4. 4

    Finish with an exit ticket

    A short wrap-up of what you covered and where you are.

Option one

Bring something

A question you couldn’t finish, a topic that didn’t land, a past paper. Bring it and we’ll work through it.

Option two

Let your coach plan it

Not sure where to start? Your coach builds a curriculum and you follow it. Get ahead of class, keep pace with it, or go beyond it.

Questions parents ask

What makes Peer Coaching different?

Your coach is a Grade 11 UWCSEA student who is strong in your course. They took it earlier if you’re in Grade 9 or 10, or take it with you now if you’re in Grade 11. They remember what was hard and what comes up in assessments. Bring something you’re stuck on, or let your coach plan the sessions. Many families use it alongside other support.

Who can sign up?

Students in Grades 9, 10 and 11, in as many subjects as they like.

Who are the coaches?

UWCSEA students who are strong and confident in their subject. Names and details aren’t on this public site. Profiles are in a slideshow for the school community.

When do sessions run?

Five slots, Monday to Friday: 7:30–8:00 am, 12:45–1:15 pm, 1:15–1:45 pm, 3:00–4:00 pm and 4:00–5:00 pm.

How big are sessions?

Small. You may be the only student in a session, or be joined by up to three others.

Is it confidential?

Yes. What’s said in a session stays there, and nothing goes to teachers as a judgement on your ability. The exception is a wellbeing or safety concern, which coaches pass to a member of staff. Every coach is briefed on this.

What is expected of students?

Turn up to slots you sign up for, or cancel in advance. Coaches never complete assessed work.

How do we sign up?

Use the sign-up form at the top of this page. Choose your subjects and slots.

What if it isn’t working out?

Tell us. Students give feedback on sessions, coaches record how they went, and coordinators use both to fix problems.

Does it cost anything?

No. It’s free.
