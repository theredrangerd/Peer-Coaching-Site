Why Peer Coaching works

Peer Coaching is run by students, for students, on a simple idea: everyone deserves to learn in a setting that is approachable and tailored to them. Your coach isn’t an outside expert. They are a student who has recently been exactly where you are.

They know your course from the inside

Grade 9 and 10 coachees are coached by students who took the same course in an earlier year. Grade 11 coachees are coached by students taking the same IB course alongside them. Either way, your coach knows which questions come up, which topics look harder than they are, and which look easier.

Among the best in their subject

Coaches are among the strongest students in the subject they coach, so they can explain a topic clearly and show you how they approach it.

Built around what you’re stuck on

You choose the subject and set the pace. Nobody moves on because the rest of the class has. Bring a question you couldn’t finish, a topic that didn’t land, or a past paper.

Questions are easier to ask

Sessions are small. You may be the only student, or be joined by up to three others, which is very different from asking in front of a class of twenty-five.

A small commitment, and free

A half-hour slot at 12:45 costs a lunch break, not an evening. Peer Coaching also costs nothing, and works alongside any other support you have.

How a session works

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Choose the subject and slot you want, in advance. Sessions are matched by subject, not assigned at random.

  2. 2

    Come prepared

    Bring something specific: an unfinished question, a topic that didn’t land, or a past paper to work through.

  3. 3

    Work through it

    Your coach works alongside you and supports your thinking.

  4. 4

    Finish with an exit ticket

    A short wrap-up to capture what you covered and where you are.

Why you won’t find names here

Our coaches are students, and many are minors. This page is open to anyone on the internet, so we don’t publish their names, photos or results here. Families and students in the school community can read the coach profiles on the internal site instead.

What you can rely on

Supported by the school

Peer Coaching is supported by the Head of Learning.

Confidential, with one exception

What is said in a session stays there, and nothing is reported to teachers as a judgement on your ability. If something raises a concern about a student’s wellbeing or safety, coaches pass it to a member of staff. Every coach is briefed on this.

Academic honesty

Coaches support learning. They do not complete assessed work for anyone, and coaching stops at the line where academic honesty begins.

Revision resources, open to everyone

Whether or not you sign up, the coaches’ recommended past papers, video channels and guidance on learning with AI are there for anyone to use.

Questions parents ask

How is this different from private tuition?

Your coach is a Grade 11 UWCSEA student who excelled in your course, having already taken it if you are in Grade 9 or 10, or taking it with you now in the IB if you are in Grade 11. They remember which parts were hard and what comes up in assessments, and sessions are built around the specific thing you are stuck on. Many families use Peer Coaching alongside other support.

Who can sign up?

Students in Grades 9, 10 and 11, in as many or as few subjects as they like.

Who are the coaches?

UWCSEA students who are among the best in their subject. We don’t publish their names or details on this public site; profiles are on an internal site for the school community.

When do sessions run?

There are five slots each week: 7:30–8:00 am before school, 12:45–1:15 pm and 1:15–1:45 pm at lunch, and 3:00–4:00 pm and 4:00–5:00 pm after school. Choose the ones that fit your timetable.

How big are sessions?

Small. You may be the only student in a session, or be joined by up to three others.

Is it confidential?

Yes. What is said in a session stays there, and nothing is reported to teachers as a judgement on your ability. The one exception is a concern about a student’s wellbeing or safety, which coaches pass to a member of staff. Every coach is briefed on this.

What is expected of students?

If you sign up for a slot, turn up, or cancel in advance so your coach isn’t left waiting. Coaches support your learning but never complete assessed work for you.

How do we sign up?

Use the sign-up form linked at the top of this page. Choose the subjects you want and the slots that fit your timetable.

What if it isn’t working out?

Feedback runs both ways. Students give feedback on sessions and coaches record how they went, and the coordinators use both to resolve any friction.

Does it cost anything?

No. Peer Coaching is free.
