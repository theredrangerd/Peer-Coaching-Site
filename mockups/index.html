Peer Coaching Hub

Design review · Phase 0

One website, three directions

This is the Peer Coaching Hub shown three different ways. The colours, the content and the programme are the same in all three — what changes is how the site is structured and how it feels to move around. Open each one, click through it, then use “Back to overview” to return here.

A note from Noah

I’m Noah — a peer coach, and the person who put this together. I built it for Elena and Tim because I think a proper hub could do real good for the programme: easier to find, easier to trust, and easier to run.

I’m away at Forum and can’t be there for the review in person. Elena and Tim are the main liaisons on this, and I trust them completely to carry the process forward. I’m really looking forward to hearing what you think.

What we’re asking: which structure fits the programme best? Not which page looks most finished — these are quick mockups with placeholder text and invented people. No real student data.
