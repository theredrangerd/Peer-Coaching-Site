UWCSEA Peer Coaching

Free tutoring, student to student.

We pair students who are strong in a subject with students who want to lift their grades and build better study habits — at no cost. It’s a deliberate alternative to Singapore’s expensive private tuition: help should not depend on what a family can pay.

Good help shouldn’t have a price tag.

Private tuition in Singapore is expensive, and not every family can access it. Peer Coaching exists so a student who is struggling can get consistent, knowledgeable help from someone who sat the same courses recently — for free, on campus.

How the programme works

How it works

Three steps to your first session

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Tell us the subject you want help with and your free periods. It takes a couple of minutes.

  2. 2

    Get matched

    A coordinator pairs you with a coach who knows that course and shares a free slot with you.

  3. 3

    Meet regularly

    Most pairs meet once or twice a week during lunch or a study period, right through the term.
