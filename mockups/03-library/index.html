Peer Coaching Hub

Free, student-to-student tutoring at UWCSEA — a deliberate alternative to expensive private tuition. This is the reference for how the programme runs and the revision resources the coaches recommend.

New here? Sign up with the student form, tell us your subject and free periods, and a coordinator matches you with a coach within about a week. It costs nothing.

About Peer Coaching

Peer Coaching is a student-run programme. Trained peer coaches — usually students in Grade 11 and 12 with a strong record in their subject — run regular sessions in the subjects they know well. It is free, on campus, and built around learning how to study rather than just getting an answer.

Why it exists

Private tuition in Singapore is expensive and not everyone can access it. Peer Coaching exists so a student who is struggling can get consistent, knowledgeable help from someone who sat the same courses recently — without a price tag.

How it works

  1. Sign up — tell us the subject and your free periods.
  2. Get matched — with a coach who knows the course and shares a slot.
  3. Meet regularly — usually once or twice a week, 30–40 minutes, through the term.

FAQ

Draft Answers to confirm with coordinators.

How do I sign up for coaching?

Fill in the student sign-up form. You’ll give your subject, current level and free periods. A coordinator matches you with a coach within about a week.

How do I become a peer coach?

Use the “Become a peer coach” form. You’ll usually need a 6 or 7 in the subject at your current level and a teacher recommendation. Coaches get a short training session first.

Does it cost anything?

No. Peer Coaching is completely free — that is the point of the programme.

How much time does it take each week?

Most pairs meet once or twice a week for 30–40 minutes, usually at lunch or in a shared study period. You agree the schedule with your coach.

What actually happens in a session?

Not a lecture. Coaches work through problems with you, look at your past papers, help you plan essays or revision, and share study habits that worked for them. You bring what you’re stuck on.

What if the match isn’t working out?

Tell a coordinator. Re-matching is normal and no one takes it personally — the right fit matters more than the first pairing.

Which subjects can I get help with?

See the Subjects section above. The list shifts term to term depending on which coaches are available.

Can I get help just before an exam, not for the whole term?

Yes. Short-term help around mocks or final exams is fine — say so on the sign-up form and we’ll match accordingly.
