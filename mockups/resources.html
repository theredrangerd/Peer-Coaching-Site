Everything the coaches recommend for revising well — past papers, video channels, how to use AI to learn, and the best picks for each subject. Curated by coaches, kept honest about what each one is good for.

Draft content. The structure is final-ish; the lists are placeholders while the coaches work out what’s good for what.

How to use these

Resources help most when they support active practice, not replace it. The coaches’ rule of thumb:

  1. Find the gap. Do a past paper or a topic quiz first and see what you actually get wrong.
  2. Fill it, briefly. Use a video or a guide to unblock that one thing.
  3. Practise it again. Go straight back to questions. If you can’t do them without the video, you haven’t learned it yet.
