How to use these

Resources work best alongside active practice, not instead of it. The coaches’ rule:

  1. Find the gap. Do a past paper or topic quiz and see what you get wrong.
  2. Fill it. Use a video or guide to fix that one thing.
  3. Practise again. Go back to questions. If you can’t do them without the video, you haven’t learned it yet.

Learning with AI

Draft, not yet published. This guide must be checked against the school’s academic integrity and AI policy first.

How to use AI to learn, not to hand in. It will cover:

  • Asking for explanations at your level, then checking them against your notes.
  • Generating practice questions and worked examples.
  • Getting feedback on your reasoning, not the finished answer.
  • Where the line is, and what counts as your own work.
